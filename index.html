WHAT I BRING TO THE TABLE

Who Am I?

Beyond research, I'm an athlete — a former competitive gymnast, equestrian, and powerlifter. None of it came naturally to me. I improved at each one by showing up on the days it would've been easier to quit. It's taught me that passion and talent make success look easy, but it's the work you put in that actually builds you.

I carry that into everything I do, and I get real satisfaction from helping a business, or a person, reach somewhere they couldn't have reached alone.

I bring to the table 3+ years of qualitative research experience — agency-side across CPG, tech, healthcare, and beauty clients, and academic-side researching into how multicultural audiences engage with American sports, with direct implications for sports marketers building audience strategy. I decided to pursue an MS in Integrated Marketing Communications at Northwestern's Medill School as a deliberate investment in turning fieldwork instinct and my passion for uncovering consumer truths into helping brands that prioritize serving real consumer and market needs.

A NOTE ON AI

"How do I make the most of AI?" You're looking at the proof.

I used AI to build this website, but not by giving it free rein. While AI handled the syntax, I directed it by describing the design I wanted, catching when copy sounded like a robot had written it, pushing back on layouts that didn't fit, and flagging when a research finding was being oversimplified. It demonstrates how I use AI as part of my workflow: facilitating the mechanical parts of a process so there's more time for the parts that require creativity, thought, and the strategic inference that I bring to the table. That's my thought on how AI should be used in our lives, and I want this website to be that literal argument.
