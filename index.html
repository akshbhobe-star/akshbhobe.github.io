Who Am I?

I am curious enough to ask why, and disciplined enough to find the answer.

People often look at my Northwestern degree and see a consumer researcher. The deeper story is my background as a lifelong athlete.

Gymnastics taught me precision. Being an equestrian taught me adaptability. Powerlifting taught me discipline under pressure.

Those lessons shape how I work. I care about getting the details right, I'm comfortable when the answer isn't obvious, and I stay with a problem long enough to find the insight underneath it.

I also use AI and LLMs as part of my workflow, particularly for synthesis, prototyping, and repetitive tasks. I still own the research, the analysis, and the judgment behind the final recommendation. Used well, technology gives me more time to focus on asking better questions, challenging assumptions, and finding connections others might miss.
