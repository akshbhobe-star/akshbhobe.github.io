02 — THE ENGINE

The Engine Behind the Strategy

People look at my Northwestern degree and see a consumer researcher, but the real engine is my background as a lifelong athlete—spanning competitive gymnastics, equestrian, and powerlifting.

Powerlifting taught me absolute precision and how to execute under heavy pressure; endurance sports taught me how to pace for the long game and push through the messy middle of a long strategy cycle.
